# Brand

Brand identity, visual standards, and tone of voice for panda and tastybamboo.

## Folder Structure

| Folder | What |
|--------|------|
| `panda/` | Panda CMS brand — logo, colours, type, component style |
| `tastybamboo/` | Tastybamboo company brand — logo, colours, corporate identity |

## Key Principles

- **panda** is always lowercase (like neurobetter)
- **tastybamboo** is always one word, lowercase
- Both brands use UK English
- Panda brand should feel: approachable, developer-friendly, reliable, modern
- Tastybamboo brand should feel: professional, trusted, technical, premium
