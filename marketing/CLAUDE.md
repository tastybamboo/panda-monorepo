# Marketing

Marketing strategy, positioning, outreach, and content for the panda ecosystem
and tastybamboo as a company.

## Key Principles

- UK English always
- Professional, warm, developer-friendly — never hype or startup clichés
- Primary audience: Ruby/Rails developers, technical founders, agencies
- Secondary audience: non-technical site owners evaluating CMS options
- All claims must be substantiated — no vague "blazing fast" or "infinitely scalable"
- Competitor mentions should be factual and respectful

## Tone

- Confident but not arrogant
- Technical but accessible
- Opinionated about good defaults, flexible about customisation
- Emphasise: developer experience, Rails-native, open source core, extensible
